# Battery safety guide (CounterGuide demo document)

Demo document written for the Cloudathon proof of concept. Not an official AnyCompany policy.

## RC drive batteries: approved packs only

- Beginner RC trucks and buggies (Trailblazer HB-RC-TRUCK1, Dustrunner HB-RC-BUGGY2) must be powered by one 7.2V NiMH stick pack with a Tamiya-style connector. The approved pack is BT-NIMH-72.
- **Never power an RC car's drive motor with loose AA cells.** Do not sell loose AA cells, alkaline or rechargeable, as the drive battery for any RC kit. Loose cells in improvised holders can short, overheat, leak and cannot deliver the current the motor draws. This applies to beginner kits and to advanced cars.
- Loose AA cells are approved only for the RC transmitter (the handheld controller, HB-TX-AAPACK), which takes four AA cells.
- Do not sell a LiPo pack for a beginner kit. LiPo packs (for example BT-LIPO-2S) are only for advanced cars such as HB-RC-LIPO-ADV.

## Chargers

- Charge NiMH stick packs only with a NiMH peak-detect charger (BT-CHG-NIMH).
- Charge LiPo packs only with a LiPo balance charger (BT-CHG-LIPO), inside a LiPo-safe charging bag (BT-BAG-LIPO), and never unattended.
- Never charge a LiPo pack on a NiMH charger, or a NiMH pack on a LiPo setting.

## Damaged batteries

- Do not sell, charge or accept on the counter any pack that is swollen, punctured, leaking or hot.
- Place a damaged pack in the battery-recycling bin's sealed container and call a supervisor.

## Phones and power banks

- Use certified USB-C chargers (for example CP-CHG-USBC20).
- Power banks under 100Wh, such as EL-PWRBNK-10K, are generally carry-on friendly; customers should confirm with their airline.
