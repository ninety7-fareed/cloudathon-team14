# Returns guide (CounterGuide demo document)

Demo document written for the Cloudathon proof of concept. Not an official AnyCompany policy.

## Return windows

- Most items, including RC vehicles, chargers and electronics: **30 days** from purchase with a receipt, in original packaging.
- Cell phones: **14 days**, with all accessories, and the phone must be signed out of the customer's accounts.
- Batteries (all types, including RC packs and AA cells): **15 days if unopened**. Opened or used batteries cannot be returned for change of mind; they can only be exchanged if defective.
- Defective items: exchange within the manufacturer warranty period.

## Batteries that cannot be accepted

- Swollen, punctured, leaking or hot batteries are never accepted as returns on the counter. Follow the damaged-battery steps in the battery safety guide and call a supervisor.

## Receipt

- Without a receipt, offer store credit at the lowest recent price, with a supervisor's approval.
