# Beginner RC setup guide (CounterGuide demo document)

Demo document written for the Cloudathon proof of concept. Not an official AnyCompany policy.

## What a complete beginner RC kit contains

A beginner customer needs four things at the counter:

1. The vehicle: Trailblazer 1:18 truck (HB-RC-TRUCK1) or Dustrunner 1:16 buggy (HB-RC-BUGGY2).
2. The drive battery: one 7.2V NiMH stick pack (BT-NIMH-72). A second pack doubles run time.
3. The charger: NiMH peak-detect charger (BT-CHG-NIMH).
4. Transmitter batteries: four AA cells for the handheld transmitter only (BT-AA-ALK8 or rechargeable BT-AA-NIMH4).

Loose AA cells power the transmitter, never the truck's motor. See the battery safety guide.

## Recommended age and supervision

- Beginner trucks and buggies: ages 8 and up with adult supervision for charging.
- Advanced LiPo cars (HB-RC-LIPO-ADV) are for ages 14 and up and experienced drivers only.

## First run

1. Fully charge the NiMH pack on the peak-detect charger (about 60-90 minutes).
2. Put four AA cells in the transmitter and switch the transmitter on first, then the truck.
3. Run time is roughly 15-20 minutes per pack. Let the motor cool for 10 minutes between packs.
4. Switch the truck off first, then the transmitter.
