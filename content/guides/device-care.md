# Device care guide (CounterGuide demo document)

Demo document written for the Cloudathon proof of concept. Not an official AnyCompany policy.

## Phones

- Charge with a certified USB-C charger (CP-CHG-USBC20). Avoid leaving the phone in a hot car.
- A rugged case (CP-CASE-A15 for the Nova A15) reduces drop damage.

## Rechargeable batteries in storage

- NiMH RC packs: store partly charged in a cool, dry place; top up before use.
- LiPo packs: store at storage voltage (about 3.8V per cell) in a LiPo-safe bag (BT-BAG-LIPO).
- Remove AA cells from transmitters and remotes that will not be used for a month or more, to prevent leaks.

## Speakers and power banks

- Recharge Bluetooth speakers (EL-BTSPK-01) and power banks (EL-PWRBNK-10K) every three months if unused.
